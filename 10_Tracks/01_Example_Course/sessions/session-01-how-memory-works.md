# Session 01: how memory works

> [!tldr] In 3 lines
> Memory is strengthened by retrieving, not by re-exposure.
> Spacing reviews over days works better than cramming.
> The study method of this vault is built on these two results.

## Pretest

1. Question: Which works better a week later, rereading a text or testing yourself on it?
   - My guess: rereading, because the text stays fresh.
   - Confidence (1 to 5): 4
   - After the session: wrong. Testing won.

## Plan

Understand why testing beats rereading, and be able to explain it without notes.

## Notes

1. Rereading feels productive because the text becomes familiar.
2. Retrieval practice: trying to recall before looking. See [[Retrieval_Practice|Retrieval practice]] and the [[Test-Enhanced_Learning_(Roediger_and_Karpicke,_2006)|original experiment]].
3. Spacing: reviews spread over days beat the same time in one session. See [[Spacing|Spacing]].

## Recall without notes

Written from memory in 5 minutes before reading the notes above: testing beats rereading after a delay, and spacing beats cramming. Missing: the numbers from the experiment.
